UM-Bridge online workshop — December 7–8

Dear MATH 565 class,

I encourage you to consider the upcoming UM-Bridge Workshop, an opportunity to explore how uncertainty quantification methods connect with computational models.

The workshop will take place online on Monday, December 7, and Tuesday, December 8, 2026, from 6:00–10:00 AM Chicago time (CST) each day—13:00–17:00 CET.

The program includes an introduction to UM-Bridge, practical exercises with computational models and reproducible benchmarks, and sessions on cloud and high-performance computing. Working installations of Python and Docker are needed for the practical exercises.

This optional workshop could be especially useful if you are interested in applying the Monte Carlo and quasi-Monte Carlo methods from our course to larger scientific computing problems.

Please see the workshop website for the program and registration link.

Fred Hickernell
